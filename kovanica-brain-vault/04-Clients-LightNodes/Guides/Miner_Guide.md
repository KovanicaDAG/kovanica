# How to Mine Kovanica (Comprehensive Hardware Guide)

Welcome to the Kovanica mining ecosystem! Most networks force you to buy specialized hardware to participate. Kovanica has engineered a unique dual-mining infrastructure to ensure *any* hardware—from a gaming PC to an industrial Bitcoin ASIC—can participate and earn KOV.

Choose your hardware track below:

---

## Track A: Native Blake3 Mining (CPU, GPU, Native ASICs)

Kovanica natively secures its DAG using the **Blake3** cryptographic hashing algorithm. Blake3 is highly parallelizable, meaning modern GPUs and multi-core CPUs excel at it. 

If you have a CPU, GPU, or a native Blake3 ASIC, you will connect directly to our Go-based Stratum Pool, which validates your hashes and directly secures the Kovanica network.

### 1. Choose Your Mining Software
Download a standard Blake3 compatible miner. Popular choices include:
- **SRBMiner-Multi** (Great for AMD/NVIDIA GPUs and CPUs)
- **BzMiner** (Highly optimized for NVIDIA GPUs)
- **XMRig** (If configured with a Blake3 plugin)

### 2. Configuration & Connection
Point your miner to the official Kovanica Go Stratum Pool.

**Example SRBMiner Configuration (`start.bat` or `start.sh`):**
```bash
./SRBMiner-MULTI --algorithm blake3 --pool pool.kovanica.online:3333 --wallet <Your-Kovanica-Address>.<Worker-Name> --password x
```

**Example BzMiner Configuration:**
```bash
./bzminer -a blake3 -p stratum+tcp://pool.kovanica.online:3333 -w <Your-Kovanica-Address>.<Worker-Name>
```

### 3. Payout Structure
The Go pool utilizes a **PPLNS (Pay Per Last N Shares)** system. 
- As you submit valid hashes (shares), they are recorded.
- When the pool successfully finds a block on the Kovanica DAG, the block reward is distributed to all active miners proportional to the shares they submitted during that specific time window.
- Payouts are completely trustless and handled natively by the pool software.

## Track B: Foreign ASIC Mining (SHA-256 & Scrypt)

If you own legacy Bitcoin ASICs (like the Antminer S9) or Litecoin/DOGE ASICs (like the VolcMiner), your hardware is physically hardwired to hash SHA-256 or Scrypt. You *cannot* natively mine Blake3. 

However, Kovanica's **Smart Proxy** allows you to seamlessly earn KOV anyway.

### 1. How the Smart Proxy Works
1. You point your ASIC to our custom Kovanica Stratum Proxy.
2. The proxy secretly forwards your hashing power to a massive public pool (like Antpool or F2Pool) to mine Bitcoin or Dogecoin.
3. The BTC/DOGE generated by your hardware is paid into the Kovanica Treasury.
4. Our proxy software intercepts every share you submit, calculates its value, and instantly pays you the equivalent amount in KOV directly from the Treasury.

### 2. Configuration & Connection
Simply log into your ASIC's web interface (usually accessible via its local IP address) and update the Stratum settings:

- **Pool URL 1:** `stratum+tcp://proxy.kovanica.online:3333`
- **Worker / Username:** `<Your-Kovanica-Wallet-Address>.<Worker-Name>` *(Crucial: The proxy uses this address to send your KOV!)*
- **Password:** `x`

### 3. Payout Structure (Proxy PPS)
The Proxy operates on a **PPS (Pay Per Share)** model.
- Because your hardware is mining a massive external chain, the Proxy pays you a fixed rate of KOV for every single valid share you submit, regardless of whether a block is found.
- The default rate is dynamically adjusted based on global BTC/DOGE profitability vs. KOV emission limits.
- A background payout script (`payout.py`) runs periodically (e.g., every 10 minutes) and executes `sendtoaddress` RPC commands to deliver your KOV directly to your wallet.
