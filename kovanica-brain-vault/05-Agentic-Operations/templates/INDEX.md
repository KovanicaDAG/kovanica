## Creating a New Agent/Skill/Command

1. Copy the relevant template
2. Fill in frontmatter and body
3. Save to `agents/subagents/`, `agents/skills/`, or `agents/commands/`
4. Add entry to the respective INDEX.md table
5. Run `./scripts/sync-agents.sh` to propagate
