## Key Decisions Required (Before Execution)

| Decision | Options | Recommendation | Blocking |
|----------|---------|----------------|----------|
| **Incorporation jurisdiction** | Croatia (HANFA) vs. Lithuania/Estonia sandbox | Croatia (target market) | Entity formation |
| **Share capital** | €150k minimum (all 8 services) | Deposit €150k in escrow pre-incorp | Bank account |
| **Directors** | Min 2 (MiCA), at least 1 resident in Croatia | 2–3 directors, 1 HR resident | Fit & Proper |
| **Shareholders (>10%)** | Names, KYC, source of funds | Finalize cap table | Fit & Proper |
| **Custody model** | Own HSM + Fireblocks/Copper API | Dual: HSM for cold, partner for hot | Vendor contracts |
| **KYC provider** | Sumsub / Veriff / Onfido / Persona | Sumsub (EU, Travel Rule module) | Contract |
| **Travel Rule** | TRISA / OpenVASP / Sygna / Notabene | TRISA (open, growing adoption) | Integration |
| **DPO** | Internal vs. external | External (cost-effective for startup) | Appointment |
| **ADR scheme** | Croatian Financial Ombudsman / EU ODR | HANFA-recognized body | Registration |
| **Sandbox vs. Full** | HANFA sandbox (6–12mo) vs. full auth | Sandbox first, convert later | Timeline |
