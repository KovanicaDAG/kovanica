## 1. Qualifying Holding Thresholds (MiCA Art. 25)

| Threshold | MiCA Requirement | Assessment Trigger |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **> 10%** | Initial assessment + notification | Acquisition / increase |
| **> 20%** | Enhanced assessment | Acquisition / increase |
| **> 30%** | Enhanced assessment | Acquisition / increase |
| **> 50%** | Enhanced assessment (control) | Acquisition / increase |

**Note**: Indirect holdings aggregated. Acting in concert = aggregated.

## 6. Documentation Checklist (Per Qualifying Shareholder)

**Natural Person**:
- [ ] Completed assessment form
- [ ] Certified passport/ID
- [ ] Criminal record certificate (all jurisdictions 10yr)
- [ ] Source of funds evidence (6 months bank statements + origin)
- [ ] Source of wealth statement
- [ ] Credit report
- [ ] Tax compliance certificate (HR + residence)
- [ ] Regulatory sanctions search
- [ ] Bankruptcy search
- [ ] Conflict of interest declaration
- [ ] Consent to data processing

**Legal Entity**:
- [ ] Completed assessment form
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation/registration
- [ ] Articles of association / bylaws
- [ ] Register of directors & UBOs (>25%)
- [ ] Audited financial statements (2 years)
- [ ] Source of funds for investment
- [ ] Regulatory status in home jurisdiction
- [ ] Criminal record for all directors/UBOs
- [ ] Consent to data processing
