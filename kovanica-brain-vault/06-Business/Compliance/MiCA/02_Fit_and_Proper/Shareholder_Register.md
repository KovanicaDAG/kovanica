## 6. Maintenance Procedures

| Trigger | Action | Deadline | Responsible |
|---------|--------|----------|-------------|
| **Share transfer** | Update register, assess Fit & Proper | 5 business days | CCO |
| **New qualifying holding** | Notify HANFA (Form) | 10 business days | CCO |
| **Threshold crossing (20/30/50%)** | Enhanced assessment + notify HANFA | 10 business days | CCO |
| **UBO change** | Update UBO register | 5 business days | CCO |
| **Acting in concert formed** | Assess aggregation, notify HANFA | 10 business days | CCO |
| **Annual review** | Verify all entries, refresh KYC | Annual | CCO |
