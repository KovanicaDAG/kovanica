# Business Plan — Kovanica Protocol CASP Application

## 1. Executive Summary

**Entity**: Kovanica Protocol Ltd. (to be incorporated in Croatia)
**Regulatory Status**: Applying for CASP authorization under MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114) via HANFA (Croatia), with EU passporting rights
**Capital**: €50,000 initial own funds (MiCA Art. 55 minimum for CASP Class 2)
**Services**: Four core crypto-asset services (no portfolio management initially)
**Target Launch**: Q2 2025 (subject to HANFA authorization timeline)

## 2. Services Offered (MiCA Art. 3(1) Points 1-4, 6)

| Service | MiCA Reference | Description |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| **Exchange: Fiat ↔ Crypto** | Art. 3(1)(1) | On/off-ramp between EUR and KVNC/other listed crypto-assets via partnered payment processors and banking relationships |
| **Exchange: Crypto ↔ Crypto** | Art. 3(1)(2) | Spot trading between KVNC and other admitted crypto-assets (BTC, ETH, USDC, EURC, and MiCA-compliant utility/ART/EMT tokens) |
| **Transfer** | Art. 3(1)(3) | Custodial and non-custodial transfer of crypto-assets on behalf of clients; includes Travel Rule compliance for all transfers ≥ €0 |
| **Execution of Orders** | Art.3(1)(6) | Agency execution of client orders against internal liquidity and external venues; best execution policy applies |

**Excluded Services (Future Phases)**:
- Portfolio Management (Art. 3(1)(5)) — requires €150k own funds; deferred to Phase 2
- Crypto-Asset Custody (Art. 3(1)(4)) — outsourced to Copper (critical outsourcing)
- Operation of Trading Platform (Art. 3(1)(7)) — not applicable (OTC/agency model)
- Placing of Crypto-Assets (Art. 3(1)(8)) — not applicable
- Reception/Transmission of Orders (Art. 3(1)(9)) — covered under Execution

## 8. Risk Factors & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| HANFA authorization delay | Medium | High | Pre-submission meetings; complete documentation; 6-month buffer in timeline |
| Banking partner de-risking | Medium | High | Multiple payment processor relationships; stablecoin bridges |
| Crypto market downturn | High | Medium | Diversified revenue; low fixed cost base; staking yield buffer |
| Regulatory change (MiCA Level 2) | Medium | Medium | Active industry association participation; modular compliance architecture |
| Custody partner failure | Low | Critical | Copper due diligence; exit clause; asset segregation verification |
| Cyber attack | Medium | Critical | DORA-compliant ICT framework; penetration testing; incident response plan |
