# Fit & Proper Assessment — Directors (Senior Management)

## 1. Purpose & Regulatory Basis

**Regulation**: MiCA Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, Articles 16 & 55
**Guidelines**: EBA Guidelines on fit & proper assessments (EBA/GL/2021/06)
**National Law**: Croatian Act on Crypto-Asset Service Providers (Zakon o pružateljima usluga kripto-imovine)
**Supervisor**: HANFA (Hrvatska agencija za nadzor financijskih usluga)

**Scope**: All members of the Management Board (Senior Management) — minimum 2 directors per MiCA Art. 16(1)

## 2. Assessment Criteria (MiCA Art. 16)

| Criterion | Description | Evidence Required |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Reputation** | Good repute, honesty, integrity; no criminal convictions, regulatory sanctions, adverse findings | Criminal record certificate (Croatia + countries of residence last 10y), regulatory references, self-declaration |
| **Knowledge & Expertise** | Adequate knowledge, skills, experience for the role; understanding of crypto-assets, risks, regulation | CV, qualifications, professional certifications, track record, interview assessment |
| **Independence of Mind** | Ability to act independently, challenge, avoid conflicts of interest | Conflict of interest declaration, independence questionnaire, board evaluation |
| **Time Commitment** | Sufficient time to perform duties; no excessive external mandates | Mandate register, time commitment declaration, calendar review |
