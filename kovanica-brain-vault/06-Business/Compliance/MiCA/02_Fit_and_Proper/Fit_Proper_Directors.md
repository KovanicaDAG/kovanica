## 1. Assessment Framework

Per MiCA Art. 25–27 and EBA Guidelines (EBA/GL/2017/12), each director and senior manager must be assessed on:

1. **Reputation** — Honesty, integrity, financial soundness
2. **Knowledge & Experience** — Relevant to CASP services, finance, technology, compliance
3. **Time Commitment** — Sufficient to perform duties effectively
4. **Independence** — No conflicts impairing judgment
5. **Collective Suitability** — Board as a whole covers all required expertise

## 4. Ongoing Obligations

| Obligation | Frequency | Responsible |
|------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Re-assessment** | Annual + on material change | CCO |
| **Training (MiCA, AML, DORA, GDPR)** | Annual min. 15 hrs | HR/CCO |
| **Conflict of Interest Declaration** | Annual + on change | Each Director |
| **Criminal Record Refresh** | Every 3 years | CCO |
| **Regulatory Notification (changes)** | Within 15 business days | CCO → HANFA |
