# 🚨 BER IMPORZANT: LEGAL & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE 🚨

> **CRITICAL DIRECTIVE FOR ALL AGENTS AND DEVELOPERS**

Kovanica is operating as a payment system and MUST adhere to the strictest European Union (EU) and Republic of Croatia regulations. **Do not write code or design protocol features that violate these laws.**

## Core Compliance Pillars:
1. **MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets)**: All custody, issuance, and exchange services must be built to support MiCA compliance.
2. **HANFA & HNB (Croatia)**: The protocol must provide the necessary tooling (like Multisig RFC-001) for VASPs to register legally with HANFA and respect the Croatian National Bank's guidelines.
3. **AML & FATF Travel Rule**: The system must allow custodial endpoints to comply with the Croatian Anti-Money Laundering Act (Zakon o sprječavanju pranja novca).
4. **GDPR**: On-chain data is permanent. **NEVER** build features that store Personally Identifiable Information (PII) on the DAG.

Reference the full `LEGAL_PAYMENT_POLICY.md` for exact terms. This is a foundational, non-negotiable law of the Kovanica architecture.
