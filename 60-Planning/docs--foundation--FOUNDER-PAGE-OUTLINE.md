# Kovanica Protocol — Founder / Team Page Outline

**Content structure · Copy guidance · Legal notes**  
**Date:** September 2026 · Pre-Mainnet  
**Status:** DRAFT — content to be written before publishing

---

## 1. Purpose of the Page

Give visitors, developers, node runners, and future partners a clear, honest answer to "Who is behind Kovanica?". Reduce anonymity risk while protecting personal safety. Signal that the project has a real steward and a path to Foundation governance.

---

## 2. Recommended URL & Placement

- Primary: `https://kovanica.online/founder` or `/about` or `/team`
- Also linked from footer of explorer, wallet, docs, and playground
- Short version can live on the landing page hero or "About" section

---

## 3. Page Structure (Sections)

### 3.1 Hero

- Headline: "Built by [Name], stewarded by the Kovanica Foundation"
- One-sentence purpose of the chain
- Optional: professional portrait (or abstract brand mark if privacy preferred)

### 3.2 Founder Bio (short)

- Name (or public handle if preferred)
- Role: Founder & Protocol Steward
- Background: 3–5 lines (relevant experience, why Kovanica exists)
- Location (country or region is enough)
- Statement of commitment: long-term stewardship + progressive decentralisation

### 3.3 Foundation Status

- Current status: "Foundation in formation" / "Registered under …"
- What the Foundation will hold: domains, trademarks, treasury, IP
- Link to the public legal skeleton / roadmap when published

### 3.4 Social & Contact

- X / Twitter (primary public channel)
- GitHub (KovanicaDAG org)
- Email: `founder@kovanica.online` or `contact@kovanica.online`
- Optional: Telegram / Discord for community (moderated)
- **Never** put private phone or home address

### 3.5 Transparency Notes

- Link to RFC-006 tokenomics (premine + treasury clearly stated)
- Link to Master Roadmap / planning pack when public
- Short paragraph: "Early control is intentional and documented; the goal is progressive decentralisation under transparent rules."

### 3.6 Optional Sections

- "Why I started Kovanica" (personal motivation, 1 short paragraph)
- Timeline / milestones already achieved
- Call for collaborators (developers, node runners, legal counsel)

### Hero

### Founder line

### Foundation line

> The Kovanica Foundation is being established to hold domains, trademarks, treasury, and protocol IP. Its purpose is to provide legal continuity and a clear path from founder-led development to broader governance.

---

## 5. Legal & Safety Notes

- Do not publish private keys, backup phrases, or exact home address
- Use a dedicated project email, not personal email
- Once the Foundation exists, move domain registrant and GitHub org ownership to the entity
- Consider a simple privacy policy for any contact form
- If you prefer relative anonymity, use a consistent public handle + clear Foundation language instead of full legal name
